Convert Font (TTF/OTF, in browser) Beta

Runs in this browser tab — on your phone or computer, not on the reader — then uploads one finished file automatically. No separate app, no PC-side script. Pick a folder containing the font's style files (TTF/OTF) — Regular, and optionally real Bold/Italic/Bold Italic files (not synthesized), get matched by filename automatically and bundled into a single multi-style .cpfont the reader picks the right style from on its own. No kerning/ligatures still (needs GPOS/GSUB table parsing, out of scope here).

…or pick a .zip archive instead (e.g. downloaded straight from Google Fonts) — its TTF/OTF files are extracted automatically:

"Comprehensive" requests a large span of scripts and symbol blocks, but only glyphs your font actually has get rasterized — a Latin/Cyrillic font stays fast, since everything else is skipped after a quick existence check. Fonts with real CJK/Hangul coverage can take a while (thousands of glyphs per style) — stay on this page until you see a result. Needs a reasonably current browser (uses the FontFace and Canvas APIs). No practical size limit here, unlike an on-device conversion — real "designer" fonts (Bookerly, Amazon Ember, Literata, and similar) work fine. Converts and uploads one .cpfont per size (12/14/16/18pt plus anything you add above) — each upload happens in turn, so this can take a while with several sizes. Custom range entries can be a single codepoint or a first-last span, with or without 0x/ U+ prefixes.
